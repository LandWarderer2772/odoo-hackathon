## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance dark aesthetic tailored for next-generation institutional wealth, algorithmic treasury management, and high-frequency fintech operations. Moving away from standard flat interfaces, the system immerses users in an abyss of deep obsidian, midnight slate, and deep-space navy canvases pierced by luminous electric blue, cyan, and neon accents.

The visual direction centers on **Dark Glassmorphism & Cyber-Luminescence**:
- **Deep Space Obsidian Surfaces**: Multi-tiered dark field substrates (`#050b14`, `#0a1120`, `#0f172a`) replace standard flat darks, eliminating eye fatigue while conveying prestige, institutional solvency, and infinite depth.
- **Cryogenic Glass Overlays**: Semi-transparent frosted glass modules (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`) with high-refraction edge bevels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and targeted azure rim lights.
- **Electric Vector Glows**: Laser-focused kinetic glows, luminous data traces, and glowing status pills evoke active fiber-optic networks and liquid digital capital.
- **Tactile Ergonomics**: Precision-milled pill and pebble geometries maintain a tactile, hardware-inspired finish across dense data tables, interactive charting surfaces, and modal sheets.

## Colors

The dark color system relies on high-order chromatic contrast between cryogenic deep-space backgrounds and hyper-vibrant optical accents.

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Electric Royal Blue. Powers core conversion states, high-priority interactive nodes, ledger focus triggers, and dominant chart areas.
- **Secondary (`#0A1120`)**: Midnight Slate. Serves as the structural chassis for cards, side navigation rails, container docks, and recessed structural grouping.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`) & Neon Accent (`#00F0FF`)**: Luminous Cyan & Radiant Photon. Used for high-frequency micro-indicators, positive yield vectors, volumetric glow sources, and focus states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#050B14`)**: Obsidian Void. The root viewport baseline, offering absolute deep blacks with just enough cold tint to prevent dead pixels.
- **Atmospheric Glass System**:
  - `glass-panel`: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` provides high-contrast text backing with backdrop blur filtering.
  - `glass-stroke`: Subtle 1px structural rim light using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - `glass-stroke-glow`: Dynamic active edge highlight using `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **Semantic Signals**:
  - Positive Yield / Credit: `#10B981` (Vibrant Emerald) with cyan undertones.
  - Expense / Volatility / Drawdown: `#F43F5E` (Laser Crimson).
  - Escrow / Pending / Alert: `#F59E0B` (Luminous Amber).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes sharp optical contrast and absolute clarity against near-black glass foundations. **Plus Jakarta Sans** is retained to ensure high humanist geometry while maintaining crisp edges in dark environments.

- **Contrast Hierarchy**:
  - Primary Headers & Balances: Render in high-clarity pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale iced cyan (`#F0F9FF`) with bold weights to maintain optical weight without bleed.
  - Body Content: Rendered in muted slate tones (`#94A3B8`) to eliminate high-frequency eye fatigue across dense banking views.
  - Micro Footnotes & Inactive Metadata: Set in deep steel slate (`#64748B`).
- **Tabular Figures & Currency Realism**: All data elements depicting liquidity, ticker counts, FX rates, and timestamps must strictly declare `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` to prevent layout shift during realtime balance mutations.
- **Negative Kerning**: Display sizes employ aggressive negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for a unified, mechanical presence across portfolio summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an expansive 12-column desktop fluid grid engine designed for information density, real-time analytics, and visual depth.

- **Desktop Framework**: Max container width of 1440px with a baseline `3rem` section margin and `1.5rem` structural gutters, allowing nested glass modules to breathe against the midnight backdrop.
- **Multi-Screen Responsive Matrix**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with persistent frosted side navigation, central multi-panel telemetry cards, and right-rail transactional feeds.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with collapsed icon-rail navigation and stacked telemetry modules (`gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`).
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with docked glass bottom sheets and vertically stacked metrics (`gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`).
- **Grouping Discipline**: Atomic items within actions and pills cluster with `space-xs` (6px) or `space-sm` (12px), while large dashboard telemetry cards preserve `space-lg` (32px) to prevent bleed from neighboring ambient glowing glows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous transparency, layered frosted surfaces, and colored ambient glows rather than standard black dropshadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Void)**: Root backdrop `#050B14`, occasionally washed with subtle radial gradients of deep indigo (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)` blur 120px) to simulate dimensional ambient backlighting.
- **Level 1 (Structural Midnight Cards)**: Background `#0A1120` or `#0F172A` at 80% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bound by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, and anchored with `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Frosted Slates & Modals)**: Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`, framed by a dual border:
  - Outer: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - Inner Highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
  - Shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(5, 11, 20, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused & Hover Components)**: Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.75)` with vibrant edge lighting: `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)` and ambient halo `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -4px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25), 0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Laser Bevel Sheen**: Elevated elements utilize an asymmetrical top-to-bottom edge sheen: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.01) 100%)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry, softening analytical banking data through physical pebble aesthetics and continuous curvature:

- **Buttons & Control Pills**: Full radius (`9999px`) for action triggers, time-range selectors, transfer triggers, and identity status tags.
- **Card Shells & Dashboards**: Large corner radius (`1.5rem` to `2rem`), turning analytical dashboards into ergonomic, physical frosted glass tiles.
- **Internal Micro-Containers**: Small inputs, nested metric trays, and icon containers use `0.75rem` to `1rem` to maintain smooth nesting with parent shells.
- **Graph & Vector Curvature**: Smooth bezier spline curves for monetary analytics, with glowing line widths of 2.5px to 3px finished with rounded stroke caps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber-Action**: Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), styled with an electric gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #38BDF8 0%, #2563EB 100%)`. Content displays bold text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover triggers an intensified outer photon halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary (Dark Glass Pill)**: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` fill with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, crisp border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F0F9FF`. Hover state switches border to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`.
- **Icon Quick-Actions**: 44px circular or rounded-pill nodes with `background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, and electric cyan iconography (`#38BDF8`).

### Cards & Analytical Slates
- **Liquid Asset Card**: Rounded container (`2rem` radius) showcasing an obsidian-to-midnight gradient (`linear-gradient(145deg, #0A1120 0%, #0F172A 100%)`), overlaid with translucent cyan concentric contours. Top border features a delicate laser highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Metric Cards**: Frosted slate surfaces with small glowing micro-trends, tabular currency readouts in `#FFFFFF`, and an upper right delta chip with green/red alpha backing (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`).

### Transaction Lists
- **Rows**: Spaced glass strips with `background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`, dividing lines styled as faint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. Hover initiates a soft blue ambient edge glow.
- **Identity & Merchant Icons**: 40px circular badges in deep navy (`#0A1120`) bordered by `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)` with vibrant category icons.
- **Data Arrangement**: Counterparty in bold white (`title-md`), transaction timestamp in slate (`body-sm`), amounts in tabular bold figures (expenses in `#FFFFFF`, credits in `#38BDF8` or `#10B981`).

### Input Fields
- Floating pill-style search and transactional transfer bars.
- Base style: `background: rgba(10, 17, 32, 0.75)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text in pure white with placeholder in `#64748B`.
- Focused state: Border transforms to `1px solid #38BDF8` with an interior glow `inset 0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` and soft outer shadow `0 0 20px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Segmented Range Selectors (1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y)**: Enclosed within a unified glass pill dock. Active segment renders with bright `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)` and a distinct border highlight, while inactive items rest in muted `#94A3B8`.
- **Checkboxes**: 20px rounded squircle (`border-radius: 6px`) in `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Checked state displays a solid `#2563EB` fill with a glowing `#FFFFFF` checkmark and an outer cyan micro-halo.
